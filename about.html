Who I am

My name is jongjun Lee. I'm currently studying Computer Science Software Technology at De La Salle University

I have a variety of interests, but if I had to pick just one, I would say I enjoy organizing data. It is not so much about the act of programming or performing calculations; rather, I enjoy the process of researching material and systematically organizing that information. I am currently working with AI to build a site that organizes details—such as character relationships, development arcs, and abilities—from anime, comics, and TV dramas. There isn't a specific event that sparked this interest; I have simply always enjoyed researching things and arranging information in a neat, visually appealing way since I was young.
